# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Dependency-free HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with a Vercel-compatible serverless contact endpoint.

## Users

Business owners, operators, and leaders evaluating how operational friction affects cost, capacity, revenue, decision speed, and reliability.

## Product Purpose

The public website explains how Delaro Systems diagnoses operations, quantifies addressable problems, designs appropriate interventions, and measures outcomes. Success means a qualified visitor understands that technology follows diagnosis and can begin a credible operational conversation.

## Positioning

Delaro is an operational systems and technology consultancy. It maps how companies operate, identifies expensive friction, quantifies opportunities, and designs the simplest credible systems to improve performance. It does not begin with a predetermined technology or package.

## Operating Context

Delaro examines business structure, operating flows, ownership, systems, data, handoffs, decisions, exceptions, and economic consequences. Its governing sequence is: understand, map, diagnose, quantify, prioritize, architect, implement, measure, and improve.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Possible interventions include process redesign, integrations, automation, AI and decision support, internal applications, dashboards, reporting, and data infrastructure.
- The appropriate intervention is selected only after diagnosis.
- Client ownership, human oversight, reliability, auditability, and measurable outcomes are durable constraints.
- The public website must not invent clients, engagements, metrics, testimonials, credentials, partnerships, legal terms, or deployment status.
- The contact form requires deployment and delivery credentials before it can be operational.

## Brand Commitments

Delaro Systems uses Deep Navy, Sea Glass, Slate, Silver Gray, and Warm White; Manrope headings; Inter body text; an editorial twelve-column grid; and the Linear Horizon motif. The approved homepage is the visual authority for all public routes.

## Product Principles

- Understand the business before designing technology.
- Quantify the economic problem before recommending investment.
- Use the simplest reliable intervention capable of producing the outcome.
- Preserve appropriate human judgment, client ownership, and operational resilience.
- Define success before implementation and measure what changed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, keyboard access, readable responsive typography, explicit form labels and errors, reduced motion, reduced transparency, and increased contrast support are required.
